05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Curious?
Start here.

Simple answers before you make your first plan.

When is the best time to visit Kashmir? +

Each season has its own character. Spring brings blossom and garden walks; summer is popular for green valleys; autumn has chinar colour; and winter is known for snow experiences around Gulmarg. Conditions vary, so check forecasts close to travel.

Can I customise one of these itineraries? +

Yes — these are suggested routes, not fixed packages. Use the enquiry builder to describe your preferred dates, group and interests. The current preview copies your request rather than sending a booking.

Are the sample trips available to book now? +

Not yet. This is a website prototype. Prices, operators, accommodation partners and live availability have not been connected. No payment or reservation is processed here.

Will every place be accessible in winter? +

No. Snow, weather, road controls and local regulations can affect routes, especially in alpine areas. Check official advisories and reconfirm arrangements shortly before travelling.

Is a Gulmarg gondola ride included? +

It is not automatically included. Tickets and operating status are subject to the official provider's availability, weather and booking rules. Check the current conditions before planning.
